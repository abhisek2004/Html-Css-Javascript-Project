# 🤝 Community Guidelines & Code of Conduct

### Building a Better Community, Together.

We believe great communities are built on respect, collaboration, and shared knowledge. Whether you're contributing code, sharing ideas, or helping others, let's make this space welcoming, inclusive, and productive for everyone.

### 🌟 Our Core Principles

- **🤝 Respect Everyone** — Treat all members with kindness, professionalism, and respect, regardless of their experience or background.
- **💡 Constructive Collaboration** — Share thoughtful feedback, exchange ideas, and help fellow contributors improve and succeed.
- **🚫 Maintain a Positive Environment** — Harassment, discrimination, offensive language, spam, and inappropriate behaviour are not welcome.
- **🌱 Encourage Growth** — Support beginners, appreciate different perspectives, and create opportunities for everyone to learn.
- **🛡️ Take Responsibility** — Help maintain a safe community by reporting misconduct or violations when you encounter them.
